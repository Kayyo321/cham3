## The corrected E5 wording, for the owner

> **Erratum E5 (owner, 2026-10-14; wording corrected after M2-006-A-R3-02, owner confirmation requested).** The rule "a restriction must survive any write failure followed by a restart" holds when at least one durable mark of the restriction is made before the process dies: its ledger entry; its own durable record (the user's journal record for a per-user restriction; for a policy restriction, the anchor image that carries the policy state); or, once its ledger append has failed, the removal or emptying of the ledger's validity file or the `unverifiable` run marker. Outside the guarantee is only the case in which every mark fails at once and the process dies before any retry succeeds, followed by a same-boot restart. How the window ends depends on which write succeeds first: a successful invalidation or `unverifiable` marker makes the next same-boot start contain; a journal record that becomes durable while the ledger and the anchor still fail is rolled forward and replayed, so the restriction stays in force without containing the user. A reboot contains. (Full text: ADR 0006 §3.)
